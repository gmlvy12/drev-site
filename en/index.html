Drawing & BOM changes in your file server,
reviewed in a morning briefing

An installable watcher scans your company file server once a day and briefs what changed and how — with a photo of the changed spot and the evidence (previous value, current value, location) — kept as a change record per project. Your design team just keeps saving files as usual. Records open in the app (project list, document list, document history), and briefings are written as ready-to-send email drafts (.eml); add your company SMTP server to send them automatically.

Note: the app, the briefings and the help are currently Korean only. English UI is on the roadmap — contact us if you'd like an English pilot.

No PDM system? Never miss a change anyway

Built for design teams that live in shared folders — no behavior change required.

🗂️

Documents and change records per project

The first screen is the project list — documents, documents changed in the last 7 days, items to check and the last change for each project. Open a project for its document list (current Rev, last saved, latest change), and a document for its history. Recorded values and evidence are never rewritten.

🔍

100% change detection (watched formats)

File fingerprints (hashes) catch every change (copies that keep both size and timestamp are caught by the weekly full check), and date-suffixed saves (drawing_240901 → _241001) are automatically recognized as new revisions of the same drawing and compared.

📷

Photos and evidence

Changed spots on drawings are cropped with red boxes (before/after images when the graphics change; previous and current value plus the new revision's image when text or values change); BOMs get row-level color diffs with only the changed words highlighted, plus a purchasing-impact summary (added / removed / changed values). Renamed or added columns, moved rows, added sheets, and PDFs whose page numbers shifted are matched correctly too (drawings: PDF and DXF; DWG when a converter is installed).

⚠️

Mistake signals

Content changed but Rev not raised, Rev raised with no content change, drawing–BOM spec mismatch — suspicious signs are flagged with evidence as items to check: counts per kind on top, the list on the left, details on the right, closed with [Handled] after review. Comparing values between drawings is off by default and can be turned on in Settings.

📧

Per-project morning briefing

Every morning at the time you set (7 AM by default — waking the PC from sleep, or, if the time was missed, at the next login (or when the Drev window is opened)), each project gets a briefing as an email draft (sent automatically if an SMTP server is set) with its items to check and changed files. Projects with the same recipients are bundled into one email a day (or one per project, if you prefer). A second scan on the same day updates that day's briefing. The owner acknowledges with one click on the watch PC's screen; email recipients reply, and the owner records it — teams that don't track acknowledgements can turn it off. Companies that turn on optional LAN record viewing can let recipients view and acknowledge changes on the LAN from a link in the email.

📊

History, search & reporting

Who acknowledged what and when is kept in your own state.db (before/after images of older revisions for 90 days by default, adjustable in Settings), and the full history exports to Excel for weekly reports and approvals — or as a design change register (Excel) per project and period. "Where is M-101 used?" — search any tag or text and open the file from the results.
