No PDM system? Never miss a change anyway

Built for design teams that live in shared folders — no behavior change required.

🔍

100% change detection

File fingerprints (hashes) catch every change, and date-suffixed saves (drawing_240901 → _241001) are automatically recognized as new revisions of the same drawing and compared.

📷

Before/after evidence

Changed spots on drawings are cropped with red boxes as before/after images; BOMs get row-level color diffs with only the changed words highlighted, plus a purchasing-impact summary (added / removed / changed values). Renamed or added columns, moved rows, added sheets, and PDFs whose page numbers shifted are matched correctly too.

⚠️

Mistake signals

Content changed but Rev untouched, drawing–BOM spec mismatch, spec conflicts between drawings — suspicious signs are flagged with evidence before they become accidents.

📧

Per-project morning briefing

Every morning at the time you set (7 AM by default — waking the PC from sleep, or catching up when it boots later), each project gets a briefing: "N changes + M to review." A second scan on the same day updates that day's single briefing. The owner acknowledges with one click on the watch PC's screen; email recipients reply, and the owner records it.

🔎

Tag & full-text search

"Where is M-101 used?" — search any equipment tag or text across all drawings and BOMs, then open the file right from the results.

📊

History & reporting

Who acknowledged what and when is kept in your own state.db (before/after images of older revisions for 90 days by default, adjustable in Settings), and the full history exports to Excel for weekly reports and approvals.
